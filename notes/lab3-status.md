## IMU axes on this arm (measured 23 Oct, over USB serial)

A test build of week 2 step 2 printing the raw readings
(`tools/lab3-guide/out/src/axis_test/`, not committed) gave, arm held still:

| Arm | ax (g) | ay (g) | az (g) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Hanging, 6 o'clock | +1.08 | −0.06 | −0.24 |
| Level, 3 o'clock | +0.10 | +0.04 | +0.81 |
| Straight up, 12 o'clock | −0.94 | −0.01 | −0.21 |

So, for edit 2.1 of week 2:

    #define ARM_AXIS   0
    #define UP_AXIS    2
    #define GYRO_AXIS  1
    #define ARM_SIGN  -1.0f   // x points toward the hinge
    #define UP_SIGN    1.0f
    #define GYRO_SIGN  1.0f   // checked: gy positive while the angle rises

The accelerometer has offsets of about **x +0.07 g, z −0.22 g** (z reads −0.2 g
whenever the arm is vertical). Without subtracting them the angle is off by up
to ~12°. The test build subtracts them before smoothing:

    #define ACCEL_X_OFFSET  0.07f
    #define ACCEL_Z_OFFSET -0.22f
    float arm_now = ARM_SIGN * (fix2float15(acceleration[ARM_AXIS]) - ACCEL_X_OFFSET) ;
    float up_now  = UP_SIGN  * (fix2float15(acceleration[UP_AXIS]) - ACCEL_Z_OFFSET) ;

Gyro bias at rest: gy about −2 °/s, gz about −4 °/s.
